# Robot Sandwich: Peanut Butter and Jelly

## Assumptions

- The robot is standing in front of a clean kitchen counter.
- The robot's hands are empty and clean.
- A loaf of sliced bread is inside a closed plastic bread bag on the counter.
- A closed jar of peanut butter is on the counter.
- A closed jar of jelly is on the counter.
- A clean plate and a clean butter knife are on the counter.
- All food containers are within the robot's reach.

## Instructions

1. Pick up the plastic bread bag.
2. Untwist the twist tie that is closing the bread bag.
3. Remove the twist tie from the bread bag.
4. Pull the opening of the bread bag apart.
5. Remove one slice of bread from the bag.
6. Place the slice of bread flat on the plate.
7. Remove a second slice of bread from the bag.
8. Place the second slice of bread flat on the plate beside the first slice.
9. Pick up the jar of peanut butter.
10. Turn the peanut butter jar lid counterclockwise until the lid comes off.
11. Place the peanut butter jar lid on the counter.
12. Place the peanut butter jar on the counter.
13. Pick up the butter knife by its handle.
14. Insert the blade of the butter knife into the peanut butter.
15. Scoop a small amount of peanut butter onto the blade.
16. Remove the knife from the peanut butter jar.
17. Spread the peanut butter across the top surface of the first slice of bread.
18. Place the butter knife on the plate.
19. Put the lid back on the peanut butter jar.
20. Turn the peanut butter jar lid clockwise until it is closed.
21. Pick up the jar of jelly.
22. Turn the jelly jar lid counterclockwise until the lid comes off.
23. Place the jelly jar lid on the counter.
24. Place the jelly jar on the counter.
25. Pick up the butter knife by its handle.
26. Insert the blade of the butter knife into the jelly.
27. Scoop a small amount of jelly onto the blade.
28. Remove the knife from the jelly jar.
29. Spread the jelly across the top surface of the second slice of bread.
30. Place the butter knife on the plate.
31. Put the lid back on the jelly jar.
32. Turn the jelly jar lid clockwise until it is closed.
33. Pick up the slice of bread with peanut butter on it.
34. Turn the peanut-butter side toward the jelly-covered side of the other slice.
35. Place the peanut-butter-covered slice directly on top of the jelly-covered slice.
36. Release the top slice of bread.

**Done When:** Two slices of bread are stacked together on the plate with the peanut butter and jelly between the slices.
